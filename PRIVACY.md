# Flick Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

Flick is a command palette for Chrome. It is built to work entirely in your browser: **Flick does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, analytics, tracking, or ads, and Flick has no server.

## What Flick stores, and where

| Data | Where it's stored | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Your shortcuts and snippets | `chrome.storage.sync` — your Chrome profile, synced by Chrome to your other signed-in devices if you have Chrome Sync on | So your shortcuts follow you between devices |
| Recently used commands | `chrome.storage.local` — this device only | To show "Recent" when you open the palette |

You can export, import, or delete all of this from Flick's Settings page. Uninstalling Flick removes it.

## What Flick reads, and only when you use it

- **Open tabs** (titles and addresses) — to let you search and switch to them. Read when you open the palette; never stored or sent anywhere.
- **Bookmarks** — to show matching bookmarks in search, and to bookmark a page when you ask.
- **Browsing history** — *only if you turn on history search* in Settings → Features, which asks for Chrome's permission first. Used to show matching pages in search; never stored or sent anywhere. You can turn it off at any time.
- **The current page** — only after you open Flick on it (with your keyboard shortcut or the toolbar icon). Flick then shows its palette there, and runs the tool you choose: for example, copying the page's address, taking a screenshot, or picking a color.
- **The current site's cache** — cleared only when you run the "Clear Cache" tool, and only for that site.

Screenshots are saved to your Downloads folder and nowhere else.

## Third parties

Flick's Settings page and side panel load the DM Sans and JetBrains Mono fonts from Google Fonts. That request goes to Google like any web font request (see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)); no data about your use of Flick is included. The palette shown on web pages loads no external resources.
